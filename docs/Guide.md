## Generator :id=generator

Your “Film.csv” file should look like this:


![Middle](Images/Film.png ':size=500')


•    Your barcode value

•    The product number/type in the simulation

•    The dimensions

•    The colors

•    The time between each product appearance


Then, in the “Authorization Tag,” enter your PLC tag directly and select your PLC from the tab on the right.

![Middle](Images/Author_tag.png ':size=800')


Then, in “File Sequence,” click on the folder and open your “Film.csv” file.

![Middle](Images/Seq_file.png ':size=800')


Then click OK.

You need a different film for each generator.
